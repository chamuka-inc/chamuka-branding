# Colour and contrast

Ember `#E24E1B` is the master primary. It **fails WCAG AA** for small text on paper (3.74:1) and passes AA large. Use it for buttons, marks, and display — not body copy. Button labels are 16px (14px minimum, medium weight).

Ink on paper is 17.30 AAA. Slate on paper is 8.18 AAA. Muted on paper is 5.54 AA. Gold on paper fails; gold on ink is 9.47 AAA. Success on paper is 4.12 — large text / icons only.

Dark theme sits on ink. Primary lifts to `#F06A3A`. Accent (links) lifts to `#7FA0D4`. Filled slate may still use `#2B4C7E`.

Do not invent campaign palettes. Product accents (coral, forest, mineral, ember, slate) colour the name, the app-icon field, and selected controls — never the sun.
