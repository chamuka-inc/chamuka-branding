# Content and voice

Chamuka speaks to business teams. Playful, warm, African-rooted — not toy-like.

- Plain first. One sentence for what a thing is.
- Warm, not loud. No trailing exclamation marks in chrome, no emoji in org listings, no “best”.
- Adult play: Play the product can be lighter in its own UI; Play the listing stays adult.
- Named, not nicknamed. Do not drop “Chamuka” from a lockup.
- Buttons are verbs: *Save*, *Send invite*, *Create form*.
- Avoid “simply”, “just”, “awesome”, “unlock”, “magic”.

Repo naming and description tone: `templates/github-repo-naming.svg` and `brand/docs/voice.md`.
