# Content and voice

**Chamuka Labs is an applied AI lab: we ship AI-native products, put research into practice, and build tools for AI agents.**

Daybreak is brand infrastructure, not a product. Audience: business teams. Feel: playful, warm, African-rooted — not toy-like.

Voice: founder-led, short, specific, slightly dry; a tinkerer speaking for the lab.

- Lead with the lab, then name the outputs. DrawIt is the flagship. FormIt, Play, Waypoint, Vmette and others are experiments or infrastructure, not launched products. Daybreak is brand infrastructure, not a product.
- Lab / org surfaces use the master lockup. Flagship surfaces use Chamuka + DrawIt (ember `#E24E1B`).
- “From thoughts to form” is DrawIt’s line only. Do not invent a lab slogan, and do not put the positioning line under a lockup.
- “Research” means applied engineering only — no papers, benchmarks, evaluation work, or research results.
- Do not claim other chamuka-inc repos are already open source. This repository being Apache 2.0 is fine to state.
- Do not invent customers, logos, testimonials, or metrics.
- Plain first. One sentence for what a thing is.
- No trailing exclamation marks in chrome, no emoji in org listings, no “best”, no “groundbreaking”.
- Named, not nicknamed. Do not drop “Chamuka” from a lockup.
- Buttons are verbs: *Save*, *Send invite*, *Create form*.
- Avoid “simply”, “just”, “awesome”, “unlock”, “magic”.

Repo naming and description tone: `templates/github-repo-naming.svg` and `brand/docs/voice.md`.
