# Typography

Sora for display and headings. IBM Plex Sans for UI and body. IBM Plex Mono for code and tabular numbers.

- Display 56/64 600 · H1 40/48 600 · H2 32/40 600 · H3 24/32 600 · H4 20/28 500
- Body 16/24 400 · Small 14/20 · Caption 12/16 · Code 14/22 Mono

Titles in ink. Secondary lines in muted. Ember and gold are not paragraph colours. Product names in a lockup are IBM Plex Sans Medium at 0.72 of the wordmark, in the product accent.

Measure: 45–75 characters for reading text. Do not letterspace body copy.
